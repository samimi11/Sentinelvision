🎯 Mission statement

Build the Canadian answer to digital warfighter vision

Problem

Dismounted troops need fused awareness across day, night, smoke, urban interiors, and drone-contested battlespaces, while Canadian procurement also wants domestic integration, allied interoperability, and privacy-aware sensing.

Solution

Sentinel Vision AI combines helmet-mounted digital vision, AI overlays, passive radio-frequency spatial sensing, and secure networking on an operationally proven Galvion helmet base for CAF, Arctic, and NATO-relevant missions.
